Xenogerm Trader Stock

[h1]About[/h1]
In vanilla RimWorld with Biotech, the only way to get a specific xenotype is to collect individual genes and assemble a xenogerm yourself, a slow and expensive process. Xenogerms exist as items but traders never sell pre-made ones for preset xenotypes. This mod fixes that by adding complete xenogerms to trader inventories, letting you purchase a ready-made xenotype package instead of hunting down each gene individually.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Xenogerm Trading[/h2]
[list]
[*]Orbital exotic goods traders stock xenogerms for all preset xenotypes (Hussar, Impid, Sanguophage, and more)
[*]Purchased xenogerms assign the actual xenotype on implantation, enabling ideology recognition and creating "naturalized" members of endogene-only xenotypes
[*]Cheaper xenogerms appear more frequently, while expensive archite xenotypes like Sanguophage are rarer finds
[/list]

[h2]Pricing[/h2]
Xenogerm prices are based on gene complexity, metabolism impact, and archite gene count, e.g:
[list]
[*]Pigskin, Dirtmole: ~1,500 silver
[*]Hussar, Highmate: ~1,600 to 1,850 silver
[*]Sanguophage: ~3,200 silver
[/list]
Sell prices are intentionally low (5%) to prevent buy-sell exploits, with a bonus for archite xenogerms. The full formula is configurable in mod settings.

[h2]Mod Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Include archite xenotypes[/b] - Toggle xenogerms containing archite genes (default: on)
[*][b]Include inheritable xenotypes[/b] - Toggle germline xenotypes like Impid and Yttakin (default: off)
[*][b]Implant germline xenotypes as endogenes[/b] - Write a germline xenogerm's genes into the pawn's germline, so children inherit them and later implants stack instead of overwriting (default: off; requires the setting above)
[*][b]Include player-scenario xenotypes[/b] - Toggle the custom xenotypes your starting colonists were created with (default: on)
[*][b]Xenogerm Pricing[/b] - Customize the price formula by adjusting the base preset value and multipliers for metabolism, complexity, and archite genes
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Requires the Biotech DLC
[*]Fully supports the Gene Trader mod: if installed, its orbital gene trader will also stock xenogerms
[*]Safe to add or remove mid-game
[*]Automatically includes xenotypes added by other mods
[*]Vanilla Races Expanded - Android: android xenotypes are never sold (they are machines, not genelines) and stay out of the settings grid
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Q: Do I need the Biotech DLC?[/b]
A: Yes. This mod adds xenogerms for Biotech's xenotype system.

[b]Q: Will this work with my other xenotype mods?[/b]
A: Yes, xenotypes from other mods are automatically included.
